# Club record checks

Checked 27 September 2026. The demo is a New Zealand incorporated society operating under the 2022 Act. These checks flag missing evidence and recorded dates. They do not certify legal compliance, officer eligibility, child safety, accounting accuracy or the validity of a notice.

| Rule | What the command checks | Source and limits |
|---|---|---|
| MEMBER-CONSENT | Active members missing a consent date or evidence reference | [NZ records guidance](https://www.is-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/running-your-incorporated-society/records-you-should-keep/). A stored reference needs a real original document. |
| REGISTER-DETAILS | Current/lapsed members missing joining date or email | Same source. Email is this base's contact channel; a missing email prompts review, not a legal conclusion that postal contact is invalid. |
| OFFICER-EVIDENCE | Current officers missing consent or eligibility evidence | [NZ officer guidance](https://www.is-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/running-your-incorporated-society/committees-and-officers/). Re-elected officers need fresh consent and certification. Record a new appointment. |
| MEETING-MINUTES | Held meetings missing minutes references | NZ records guidance. Includes AGM minutes. |
| MEETING-NOTICE / NOTICE-RECORD | Missing or late notice against the stored period | The club constitution, recorded in obligations.source_url. Fourteen days is fictional demo policy, not a universal legal rule. |
| DUE-RECORD | Uncompleted obligations past their recorded deadline | Each obligation must identify its own jurisdiction and source. |
| COMPLETION-EVIDENCE | Completed obligations with no supporting evidence | Local record-quality rule. |

## AGM preparation

NZ societies generally must hold an AGM within six months of their balance date. Set the actual deadline as an obligation, including any applicable first-meeting exception or other statutory limit after checking your society's position. The agenda should cover the annual report, financial statements and disclosed interests. [Companies Office meeting guidance](https://is-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/running-your-incorporated-society/holding-meetings/). The interests register records disclosures and their management, but cannot discover undisclosed interests.

For Australian clubs, configure jurisdiction and obligations to the applicable state and constitution before using the checks. The NZ evidence checks are disabled when the club profile is set to an Australian jurisdiction. Victoria's associations generally lodge an annual statement within one month of the AGM, with an exemption for ACNC-registered charities. Record the due date, source and exemption decision yourself. [Consumer Affairs Victoria](https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/clubs-and-fundraising/incorporated-associations/running-an-incorporated-association/annual-reporting/step-by-step-summary). No uniform Australian notice or reporting rule is assumed.

Receipts and expenses are operational records, not a statutory set of financial statements. Payment commands record money already received; they never move funds. Training targets are club policy, not statutory coaching or safeguarding certification.
